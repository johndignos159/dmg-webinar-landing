Which lane should I pick?

Dispatching has the lowest barrier — an entity, systems, and carrier clients. Carrier needs authority, insurance, and BOC-3. Broker adds a $75,000 BMC-84 bond. Forwarder needs authority, a bond, and insurance. Different capital, different risk, same starting point.

Do I really need an LLC?

You can operate as a sole proprietor. It means your personal assets and your business are legally the same thing. In an industry where one accident can produce a claim larger than most people's net worth, that is a hard risk to justify.

How does the money actually work?

A shipper pays to move freight. A broker matches it to a truck and keeps roughly 15%. The carrier hauls it, owns the revenue, and pays the driver. A dispatcher books for the carrier and earns 5-10%. Every seat gets paid — the question is which one you want to own.
